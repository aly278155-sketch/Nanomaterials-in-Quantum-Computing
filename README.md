# Nanomaterials in Quantum Computing

## Overview
This project explores the role of nanomaterials in building advanced quantum computing systems.

Prepared by:
**Ali Hussein Allawi**  
University of Al-Qadisiyah – College of Science  
Department of Medical Physics

## Topics Covered
- Superconducting thin films
- Josephson junction interfaces
- Semiconductor quantum dots
- 2D and 1D nanomaterials
- Topological materials
- Quantum hardware challenges
- Future research directions

## Key Technologies
- Quantum Computing
- Nanotechnology
- Spin Qubits
- Superconducting Qubits
- Semiconductor Heterostructures
